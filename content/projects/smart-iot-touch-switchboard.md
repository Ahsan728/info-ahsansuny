This is an IoT smart touch switchboard where there are some special features including the load management, energy consumption of outlet, touch switch etc. This 4 gang switchboard circuit and 3D prototype is designed and manufactured inhouse lab. TTP223 touch sensor was used to sense the touch and switching the loads through relay. ACS712 current sensor measures the current of total load and Real time monitoring of energy consumption through ESP8266 connecting with internet apps and web.
