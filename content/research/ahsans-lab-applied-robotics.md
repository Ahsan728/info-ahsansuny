At Ahsan's Lab we built practical, affordable systems for problems around us: drain-cleaning and floating waste-collecting robots, IoT health wearables, biometric exam management and smart agriculture. The work led to papers at IEEE TENSYMP, ICECE, ICECTE, ICISET, ICMIME and a Springer chapter.
